Featured Projects

Investigating Public Address Systems: The Effect of Circuit Resistance on Sound Intensity

Independent Research | October 2024 – January 2025

The Context: After noticing stark inconsistencies in the volume and clarity of Public Address (PA) systems across various schools in Calgary, I set out to investigate how to better regulate sound output. Ensuring these systems remain within safe human hearing thresholds (under 70 decibels) is critical for student and staff safety.

The Investigation: To test a plausible mitigation method, I designed and executed a 5x5 laboratory trial exploring the addition of resistors into speaker circuit loops. This paper analyzes the direct relationship between circuit resistance and speaker sound intensity, proposing practical applications for safer, more consistent audible communication.

Applying Fourier Transforms to the Denoising of an Accelerometer Signal

Independent Research | October 2025 – February 2026

The Context: Signals are essential for digital communication in various facets of our life. I noticed that the Inertial Measurement Unit on my robot had signal discrepancies in autonomous testing, and conducted this research project to analyze how these discrepancies could be mitigated.

The Investigation: Investigated the definition of the Fourier Series and Discrete Fourier Transforms. Used Python to apply Fourier Transforms onto open-sourced, 3-axis accelerometer data to denoise the signals.
